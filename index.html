Understanding Payment Platform Fees

Navigating the gig economy, freelancing, or running an e-commerce store requires a strong grasp of your profit margins. A significant factor that eats into your earnings is the transaction fees charged by payment gateways and freelance platforms. FeeCalc is designed to help you quickly understand exactly how much you are paying to platforms like Stripe, PayPal, and Upwork, allowing you to price your products or services accordingly.

Detailed Breakdown of Fee Structures

Stripe: E-commerce & SaaS Standard

Stripe standard pricing for domestic credit and debit card transactions in the US is 2.9% + $0.30 per successful charge. However, if you process international cards, expect an additional 1.5% fee, plus another 1% if currency conversion is required.

PayPal: The Consumer Favorite

For standard commercial transactions (PayPal Checkout), the domestic rate in the US is generally 3.49% + $0.49. Standard PayPal invoicing or "Goods and Services" payments typically sit at 2.99% + $0.49.

Upwork: Freelance Marketplace

Upwork operates on a flat 10% service fee across freelancer contracts, simplifying fee management compared to older tiered systems.
